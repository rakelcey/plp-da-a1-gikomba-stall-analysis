# Gikomba Market Stall Performance Analysis

**Name:** Nancy Wangui Kibunga 
**Cohort:**  1 Million Devs Cohort / Data Analysis Specialization 

## Summary of Business Problem
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded two weeks of daily sales and stock levels in a notebook. As a freelance data analyst, this project evaluates her sales performance, week-on-week growth, weekday versus weekend trends, and current inventory thresholds to provide data-driven recommendations on what to restock and where to focus her business operations.

## Top Three Findings
1. **Strong Weekend Sales Surge:** Weekend sales average KES 4,900 compared to weekday sales of KES 2,685, representing an 86.75% increase in daily performance on weekends.
2. **Positive Week-on-Week Growth:** Total sales grew from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, delivering a 10.66% week-on-week revenue growth.
3. **Critical Stock Depletion:** Both Tomatoes (18 kg vs 25 kg reorder level) and Sukuma Wiki (9 kg vs 15 kg reorder level) have fallen below their safety thresholds and require immediate restocking.
